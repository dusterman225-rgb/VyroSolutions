About VyroSolutions

Small businesses should not need five different tools, a stack of logins and an IT person. VyroSolutions builds one family of apps that work the same way, so what you learn in one carries to the next.

One account. Sign in once and switch between your businesses from a list. Starting a restaurant besides your contracting company? Each business keeps its own name, team and records.
Built for phones. Install it like an app. The checkout keeps working when the internet drops and catches up when it returns.
Your data is yours. Download everything whenever you want. See who did what in the activity log.
Made for people who are not tech people. Plain words, short steps and a setup checklist for every new business.

The apps

Pick the app that fits your business. Every app includes teams and roles, scheduling, an activity log and data download.

Packages and upgrades

Each app is its own monthly subscription. Add another app any time and its price is added to your plan. There is no bundle to outgrow.

Launch offer: every app is free while we launch, with no card on file. You will be told before any price starts, and you can leave and download your data first.

Upgrades

  • Photo Plus : read up to 50 receipt and invoice photos a day instead of 15. The reader fills in your inventory from a photo, and you check every line before it is saved.
  • Bring your own AI (included with the VyroAssistant): already have your own AI account? Connect it to the VyroAssistant at no extra charge.
  • VyroAssistant : an upgrade you add to a business. Ask questions about your own sales, stock, jobs and invoices, and get drafts of messages to customers. Read-only, and you accept a notice before first use.

How it works

Create your account. It takes a minute.
Create a business and pick its app. A short checklist walks you through setup.
Invite your team. Each person gets their own login and only sees what their role allows.
Add another app whenever you grow. It is a separate business with its own name, in the same account.

Careful with your information

Separated by business. One business cannot see another's records.
Roles. Owners, managers and staff see only what they need. Money and finance pages are limited to managers.
Encrypted connection. Everything travels over HTTPS.
AI you control. The help bot never sees your business records. The VyroAssistant does, only after you accept a notice, and you can use your own AI account instead.
