This notice explains what VyqilSolutions ("Vyqil", "we", "us") collects when you use the Vyqil apps (such as VyqilContractor, VyqilLandlord, VyqilRealtor, VyqilSalon, VyqilEvents, the Vyqil Network, customer portals and the Vyqil Assistant), how we use it, and your choices. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Who is who

A business that uses Vyqil (the "Business") is in charge of the information it puts about its own customers, tenants, clients and staff. Vyqil provides the tools and stores that information for the Business. If you are a customer of a Business and want to see, fix or delete your information, ask that Business first. We can help if you cannot reach them.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see them.
  • Business information you enter: customers, jobs, invoices, estimates, expenses, appointments, notes, photos and documents, and the payment methods you choose to show your customers.
  • Customer portal information: a customer's name, email, invoices, appointments and messages shared by their Business.
  • Photos and scans: if you use the camera or scan a document, the image is sent to our AI service to read it. We do not keep the image after it is read unless you save it.
  • Vyqil Assistant: your questions and the business information needed to answer them. Answers are produced by third-party AI models. We keep a short history and usage counts so the Assistant works and so we can bill credits.
  • Community: if you list your business or post a request, other Vyqil businesses can see the business name, what you offer or need, town, an approximate location (rounded to about a mile) and any phone or email you choose to share. We never share your street address.
  • Subscription payments: handled by PayPal. We receive the subscription status and a PayPal reference, not your card or bank number.
  • Crypto payments: if you use "Pay with Vyqil", we record the wallet address, amount, network and transaction ID needed to verify a payment. These are public on the blockchain.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, approximate time zone, and error logs. If you turn on alerts, a push token for your device.
  • Electronic signatures: the name you typed, your drawn signature, the time, your time zone, your device type and a fingerprint of the document you signed.

How we use it

  • To run the apps, show your data to you and the people you choose, and keep it separate from other businesses.
  • To read documents and answer questions with the Assistant.
  • To send alerts, password emails and service messages.
  • To bill subscriptions and credits, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your business data to advertise to you.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that run Vyqil: Google Firebase (login and database), Cloudflare (hosting and workers), PayPal (subscriptions), and AI model providers used by the Assistant and scanning. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Other people you choose: your customers in a portal, your staff, other businesses in the Community when you post or list.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect people, the service or our rights.
  • A sale or merger of the business, with notice to you.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. You can download your data and close your business at any time; we then delete it from active systems. Backups and legal or billing records may remain for a limited time. Signed documents are kept as long as the Business keeps them.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, download or delete your information. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, including to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), to object to or limit processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • Turn off alerts in your device settings.
  • Ask us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We may need to confirm who you are.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules that keep each business separate, and automatic sign-out. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and do not put payment card numbers, bank numbers or ID numbers in notes.

Children

Vyqil is for adults and businesses. It is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where required), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use legal safeguards for transfers.

Changes

If we make important changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
