How the Vyqil Community works

The Vyqil Community lets businesses that use Vyqil apps find each other nearby: a landlord who needs a plumber, a realtor who needs a photographer, a contractor who needs a cleaner. This page explains what is shared and what is not.

What other businesses can see

  • When you post a request: your business name, what you need, your description, the town and an approximate point on the map (rounded to about a mile), and whether it is urgent.
  • Your phone number only if you tick the box to share it. Replies can also include the phone or email the replying business chooses to type.
  • When you list your business: your business name, what you offer, the town, how far you travel, any perk you offer, and, only if you choose, your phone, license number and a note that you carry insurance.

What is never shared

  • Your street address is not part of a request. Vyqil does not track your live location. It uses the place you set, and you can change or hide it at any time.

Please do not post

  • Street addresses, door codes, key locations or when nobody is home. Give those only after you have chosen someone.
  • Payment details, bank or card numbers, passwords, or ID numbers.
  • Anything false, abusive, or that is not a real job.

Who is responsible for what

Businesses in the community are independent. Vyqil connects you; it does not hire anyone, supervise the work, or guarantee a business, a price, or the result. License and insurance information is typed in by the business itself and is not checked by Vyqil. Ask for proof before you hire. Ratings come from the person who posted the job after they mark it done. You and the business agree on the work and the price between yourselves.

Messages

Messages are only visible to the person who posted the request and the one business that replied. Keep them about the job.

Reporting and removal

Use the Report button on a request that is spam, fake or abusive. Requests that break these rules may be removed and repeat offenders may lose their community listing. You can close your own requests and hide your listing whenever you like.
