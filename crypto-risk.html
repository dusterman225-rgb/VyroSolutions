Read this before you send or accept any cryptocurrency through Vyqil, including "Pay with Vyqil" and any VYRO wallet or payment feature. Crypto is risky. Only use money you can afford to lose.

What Vyqil is and is not

  • Vyqil is software. It is not a bank, broker, exchange, investment adviser or custodian.
  • Vyqil does not hold your crypto or keys, does not guarantee any payment, and does not offer investment, tax or legal advice.
  • Crypto is not insured by the FDIC, SIPC or any government program, and is not legal tender in most places.

Key risks

  • Transactions cannot be reversed. If you send to the wrong address, the wrong network, or the wrong amount, the funds are usually lost for good. Vyqil cannot get them back.
  • Price swings. Most crypto can change in value quickly and sharply. A payment worth one amount when sent may be worth far less or more later. Stablecoins can also lose their peg.
  • Your keys, your responsibility. If you lose your wallet, seed phrase or password, or someone steals it, no one can restore your access. Never share your seed phrase. Vyqil will never ask for it.
  • Network problems. Fees can spike, transactions can be slow, fail or be dropped, and networks can be congested, halted or attacked.
  • Smart contract and technology risk. Bugs, exploits and hacks can cause loss. Wallets, bridges and exchanges can fail.
  • Scams. Fake support, fake payment requests, look-alike websites and "guaranteed return" offers are common. Check the address and amount yourself on your own wallet screen.
  • Rules can change. Laws on crypto differ by place and change often. Using crypto may be restricted or illegal where you live. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you.
  • Taxes. Receiving, sending, selling or spending crypto can create taxable events. You are responsible for records and reporting. Ask a tax professional.
  • Verification delay. Vyqil checks payments on the blockchain. A payment is only treated as received after we, and the network, confirm it. Display screens can lag.
  • Privacy. Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. Anyone may be able to see a wallet address and its history.
  • Refunds and disputes. There are no chargebacks. Refunds are up to the business that was paid and require a new transaction.

Fees

Network fees go to the network, not to Vyqil. Vyqil may charge a fee of 2% on a Pay with Vyqil payment, shown before you confirm. Exchange or wallet providers may charge their own fees.

Test mode

Anything shown as "TEST MODE" uses no real money. Do not send real crypto to a test screen.

Your acknowledgment

By using a crypto feature in Vyqil you confirm you have read and understood this disclosure, that you accept these risks, that you are an adult, and that you are not using crypto in a place where it is prohibited. Vyqil may change, limit or stop crypto features at any time.
